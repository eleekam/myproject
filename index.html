HTTP & HTTPS Explained

A beginner-friendly guide to how browsers communicate with websites—and why secure connections matter.

What happens after you enter a website address?

Your browser sends a request, a server responds, and information travels across the internet. HTTP defines this exchange. HTTPS adds a protected connection using TLS.

Why Does Web Security Matter?

We use the internet every day for shopping, banking, school, and communication. Understanding how websites handle our information helps us recognize why secure connections are important.

Online Shopping

HTTPS helps protect payment information while it travels to a website.

Online Banking

Encrypted connections help protect financial information in transit.

Personal Accounts

Secure connections help protect passwords and account information during transmission.
